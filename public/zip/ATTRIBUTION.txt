The files in this folder (0.json to 9.json) list US ZIP codes and the city each belongs to.
They are derived from the GeoNames postal code dataset, https://www.geonames.org
(https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/US.zip, downloaded 2026-10-06), licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: lower 48 states and D.C. only; only the ZIP code, place name and state are kept.
Built by scripts/build-zip-cities.mjs.
